# Enterprise

This framework defines the enterprise territories where Bankrate has a right to play by mapping specific enterprise business objectives to our unique value propositions. By anchoring every touchpoint in these messaging territories, we ensure that while our specific wording may shift across channels, the core ideas we communicate remain consistent, authoritative, and aligned with our brand mission.

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Business Need** | Most benefits don't move the needle on retention, engagement, or growth. Businesses need to offer something that actually improves people's financial outcomes. |
| **Core Message (Short)** | A private mortgage marketplace where lenders compete, so the people you serve don't overpay—and you offer a benefit that drives real engagement and loyalty. |
| **Core Message (Long)** | Most people only see one mortgage rate—the one their lender shows them. We bring together real mortgage offers in a private marketplace where lenders compete, driving down costs through better rates and exclusive incentives not available to the public. That means they pay less, and you offer a benefit that actually drives retention and loyalty. |

## Proof Points

### Save Your Audience Money

- **Compare real mortgage offers side-by-side:** Real offers based on each person's information so they can see what they'd actually pay, not just a single lender's rate.
- **Lenders compete for the same loan:** Multiple lenders compete for the same opportunity, helping bring costs down.
- **Exclusive incentives lower total cost:** Savings like reduced fees and closing credits—available through the private marketplace—bring down upfront and long-term costs.
- **Stay in control of lender outreach:** People choose which lenders contact them—no unwanted calls.

### Backed by Bankrate

- **Millions rely on Bankrate every month:** Over 9 million people use Bankrate each month to make informed financial decisions.
- **Proven with leading partners:** Amazon, TBD, TBD, and more rely on Bankrate to power their mortgage experiences.

### Low Lift Solution

- **Integration options:** Choose the setup that fits your business—from co-branded or white-label portals to embedded API experiences.
- **Efficient deployment:** Get started with a pre-configured setup that keeps internal lift low and speeds up time to market.
- **Pre-filled, seamless experience:** Secure API connections let you pre-populate information, reducing manual steps along the way.
- **Expand beyond mortgages:** Add savings accounts, credit cards, and other lending products as your strategy evolves.
